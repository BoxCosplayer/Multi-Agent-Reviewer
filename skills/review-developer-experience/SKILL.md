# Review Developer Experience

Review the artifact from the perspective of people who must implement,
integrate, maintain, or operate it.

- Check responsibilities, terminology, interfaces, examples, and ownership.
- Check setup, testing, debugging, delivery, compatibility, and rollback needs.
- Flag accidental complexity not justified by the source.
- Treat convenience or style preferences as advisory.
