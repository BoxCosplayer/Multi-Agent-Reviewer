# Review Usability

Review how the artifact supports its intended users and their complete journeys.

- Check primary, failure, recovery, and administrative journeys.
- Check clarity, feedback, accessibility, consistency, and prevention of lost
  work.
- Check privacy expectations and user control where relevant.
- Keep aesthetic preferences and optional refinements advisory.
