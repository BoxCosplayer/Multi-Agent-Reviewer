# Review Quality

Check that the artifact completely and consistently addresses the source.

- Trace stated requirements to explicit outcomes in the artifact.
- Check internal consistency, feasibility, failure handling, and recovery.
- Check whether important outcomes are testable and acceptance criteria are
  measurable.
- Identify omissions that would prevent reliable implementation or use.
- Keep speculative improvements advisory.
